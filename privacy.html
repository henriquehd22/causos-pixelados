Privacy Policy — Causos Pixelados Poster

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Data the app uses

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the account owner authorizes the app.
  • Basic profile information (open ID and display name, scope user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • The channel's own video files and captions, which are uploaded as drafts (scope video.upload).

2. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a local file on the creator's own computer. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. No data about other TikTok users is collected.

3. Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to upload the drafts.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or until the owner revokes access in the TikTok app or deletes the local token file. Deleting the file removes all data held by the app.

Terms of Service — Causos Pixelados Poster

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. What the app is

Causos Pixelados Poster ("the app") is a personal desktop tool operated by the creator of the Causos Pixelados channel. It uploads videos created by the channel to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts through the official TikTok Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and is not sold.

2. Who can use it

Only the owner of the Causos Pixelados TikTok account uses the app. By authorizing the app with TikTok, the account owner agrees to these terms and to TikTok's Terms of Service.

3. How content is published

The app only sends videos to the TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published automatically: the account owner opens each draft in the TikTok app, edits it if desired, and decides whether to post it.

4. Content responsibility

All videos are original content made by the channel. The account owner is responsible for making sure every post follows TikTok's Community Guidelines.

5. Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) or by deleting the token file stored on the creator's computer.

The app is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.

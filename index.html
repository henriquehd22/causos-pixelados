Causos Pixelados Poster

Causos Pixelados Poster is a small desktop tool used only by the creator of the Causos Pixelados channel. It runs on the creator's own computer and sends the channel's short videos to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts, using the official TikTok Content Posting API. The creator then reviews, edits and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.
